Acronyms & Detailed Explanations

Definitions of every major term, acronym, and metric used in this dashboard and the accompanying Excel template.

ISBN (International Standard Book Number)
A unique 13-digit commercial identifier assigned to a specific edition and format of a book. Different formats (hardcover, paperback, ebook) of the same content usually receive different ISBNs. The dashboard treats each ISBN as one “title row” for counting purposes.
BISAC (Book Industry Standards and Communications)
The subject classification system maintained by the Book Industry Study Group (BISG). Codes such as FIC019000 (Literary Fiction) or BUS071000 (Leadership) enable consistent genre/subject reporting across publishers, retailers, and data services.
UDC (Universal Decimal Classification)
An international library classification system. Many national statistical offices and UNESCO-aligned book production reports still use UDC subject groups (Generalities, Philosophy, Religion, Literature, History, etc.).
YoY (Year-over-Year)
The percentage change between the same metric measured in two consecutive years (or comparable periods). Formula: ((Current − Previous) / Previous) × 100. Positive values indicate growth in title output.
Title Output / Catalogue Volume
The total number of distinct titles (or ISBNs) published in a defined period. This is the central focus of the dashboard — it measures publishing activity, not commercial success (sales or revenue).
New Title vs Reprint / Re-edition
A “New Title” is generally the first appearance of a work (or a substantially revised edition). A reprint or re-impression is an additional printing of an already published edition. Tracking the ratio shows how much of the list is fresh content versus backlist support.
Translated vs Original Language Work
Indicates whether the published edition is a translation from another language or was originally written in the language of publication. Source Language and Source Country further describe the origin of translated works.
Imprint
A brand or trade name under which a publishing house releases books. One corporate publisher may operate multiple imprints specialized by genre, audience, or market.
Edition Number & Print Run / Impression
Edition Number distinguishes major revisions (1st edition, 2nd revised edition). Print Run or Impression Number tracks successive printings of the same edition (1st printing, 2nd printing, etc.).
Lead Time (Acquisition → Publication)
The number of days (or months) between the date rights were acquired / contract signed and the actual publication date. Longer lead times may indicate complex production or translation pipelines.
Format Medium
The physical or digital form of the published product: Print only, Digital/ebook only, Audio, or Both (multi-format release). Binding Type further details print editions (Hardcover, Paperback, etc.).
Age Category / Audience
Intended primary readership band: Adult, Young Adult (YA), Middle Grade, Children, or Educational. Used alongside genre to understand the portfolio mix.
Work Type
Structural form of the content: Novel, Short Story Collection, Essay Collection, Anthology, Monograph, Textbook, Graphic Novel, etc. Distinct from subject/genre.
Series vs Standalone
Whether a title belongs to a named series (with volume numbering) or is a complete independent work. Series titles often share branding and marketing strategies.
Debut Author
An author publishing their first book (or first book with the reporting publisher). The ratio of debut to established authors indicates investment in new voices.
Metadata Completeness
The percentage of titles that have key fields populated (subject codes, translator credit, page count, language, etc.). Higher completeness improves discoverability, reporting accuracy, and downstream analytics.
POD (Print-on-Demand)
A production method in which copies are printed only when ordered. Common for self-published and short-run titles; can inflate raw title counts relative to traditional offset print runs.
STM
Scientific, Technical, and Medical publishing — a major professional/academic segment with distinct subject coding, pricing, and distribution patterns.
How Aggregates Are Calculated in This Dashboard
  • Total Titles = number of rows / ISBNs loaded.
  • New Titles = rows where Is_New_Title = “Yes”.
  • Translated = rows where Is_Translated = “Yes”.
  • Unique Authors = distinct values in the Author column (case-insensitive trim).
  • Average Pages = mean of numeric Page_Count values (blank/zero excluded).
  • Shares % = (group count / total titles) × 100, rounded to 1 decimal.
  • YoY Growth = ((later year count − earlier year count) / earlier year count) × 100.
